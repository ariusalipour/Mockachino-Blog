Brass Log is a firearms management and logging application being developed with organisations in mind.

The goal is to make usage history, inventory, storage, maintenance, and reporting easier to manage in one place.

## What it is designed to track

### Firearm usage

Brass Log records when a firearm was used, why it was used, and the history that builds up over time.

### Storage and locations

The location model is intended to organise firearms by facility, room, or cabinet.

GPS-enabled location tracking is a future possibility rather than a current promise.

### Ammunition

The ammunition area is intended to cover batches, usage, allocation, expiry monitoring, cost management, and budgeting.

### Components and maintenance

Components can be tracked alongside inspections, maintenance schedules, replacements, parts inventory, and documentation.

## Reporting and accountability

The longer-term direction includes usage statistics, inventory reports, exports, and audit trails that help an organisation understand what has happened without relying on scattered spreadsheets.

The project is being built as a React Native and Expo application for iOS, Android, and web.

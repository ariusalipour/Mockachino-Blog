The first useful version of the Practical Shooter App is deliberately broader than a rules reader.

The rules are the foundation, but match discovery, learning tools, and scoring utilities are what make the app useful before, during, and after a competition.

The next updates will focus on how those areas share data and how the app stays helpful when a shooter is offline or working from a phone at the range.

The early Brass Log model is based on the idea that firearm management is a chain of related records.

Usage affects ammunition. Ammunition affects cost and inventory. Components affect maintenance history. Storage gives the organisation a place to account for the equipment.

Keeping those relationships visible is more useful than treating each record as a separate spreadsheet, and it gives the later reporting features something solid to build on.
